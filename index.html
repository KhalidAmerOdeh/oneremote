ISummary

OneRemote does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying personal information. The app identifies your device using an anonymous device identifier only, and does not require you to create an account.

IIInformation We Collect

DataPurpose
Anonymous device identifier To manage your free trial and prevent it from resetting if you reinstall the app
Approximate country / region (derived from IP address, not GPS) Basic usage analytics
Phone model, operating system version, app version Diagnosing bugs and compatibility issues
App usage duration, feature usage, crash and error reports Improving app reliability and performance
Connected TV brand and model (e.g. "Samsung AU7000") Improving TV compatibility and troubleshooting connection issues
Voice / text command input (AI-powered command feature) Sent to a third-party AI service to interpret your command (e.g. "open YouTube and search for X")

IIIHow This Data Is Used

  • To provide and maintain the free trial, ad-supported temporary unlock, and Lifetime purchase features fairly and reliably.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems so they can be fixed.
  • To understand which features are used and how, in order to improve the app.
  • To process natural-language voice and text commands you choose to use, by sending the transcribed text — never audio — to a third-party AI provider for interpretation.

IVThird-Party Services

Supabase

Hosts our backend database and server functions that store the anonymous device data described above.

Google Play Billing

Processes all in-app purchases and refunds. We never see or store your payment details — this is handled entirely by Google.

Google AdMob

Serves the optional rewarded advertisements you may choose to watch for temporary full access.

Groq / Google Gemini

Third-party AI providers used to interpret natural-language voice and text commands. Only the text of your command is sent — never audio recordings or other personal data.

VData We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number.
  • No precise GPS location — only an approximate country derived from IP address.
  • No audio recordings are stored or transmitted — voice input is converted to text on your device first.
  • No access to your photos, contacts, or other apps.

VIData Retention

Anonymous usage and diagnostic data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support the purposes described above. You may request deletion of data associated with your device identifier at any time by contacting us with your device identifier, which is available on the app's Settings screen.

VIIChildren's Privacy

OneRemote is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

VIIIChanges to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
