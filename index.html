ISummary

OneRemote does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or phone number. The app identifies your installation using an anonymous device identifier so that features such as the free trial, promotional access, and Lifetime unlock can be applied fairly across reinstalls. Some technical and diagnostic information is processed as described in this policy so the app can function, remain reliable, and continue to be improved.

IIInformation We Collect

The categories below describe the information processed by OneRemote and the purpose for which each category is used.

DataPurpose
Anonymous installation / device identifier To attach trial eligibility, promotional access, and Lifetime unlock to your installation without requiring an account, and to help prevent trial abuse on reinstall.
Approximate country / region (derived from IP address, not GPS) Basic usage analytics and regional diagnostics. We do not request precise location permissions.
Phone model, operating system version, app version Diagnosing bugs and compatibility issues.
App usage information (features used, session duration), crash and error logs Improving reliability, diagnosing failures, and prioritising fixes.
TV brand and model, and connection-related technical information Improving compatibility with supported TVs and troubleshooting pairing or command issues.
Purchase status and entitlement information for the Lifetime in-app purchase To unlock and restore Lifetime access. Payments themselves are handled by Google Play — we do not receive your card or bank details.
Promotional access code status To validate a code you choose to redeem, apply the associated access, and detect abuse of the promotional system.
Photo selected for the Prank feature (see Section IV) To display the specific image you selected on your paired TV. The rest of your photo library is not accessed.
Voice command input, used only when you choose the voice-command feature (see Section V) To convert your spoken input into text so it can be interpreted as a command for your TV. Voice recordings are not retained as part of your OneRemote data.

IIIHow This Data Is Used

  • To operate the app's core features — TV discovery, pairing, remote commands, input, and scheduling — on your local network.
  • To provide the free trial, the rewarded-ad temporary access option, promotional access codes, and the one-time Lifetime unlock fairly and consistently.
  • To interpret voice commands you choose to use, by converting your spoken input to text.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • To understand which features are used and how, so the app can be improved.
  • To detect misuse of trial, promotional, or purchase mechanisms.

IVPhotos & the Prank Feature

The Prank feature lets you choose an image from your device and display it on your paired TV. The image is chosen through your device's standard system photo picker. OneRemote receives only the specific image you select through that picker — it does not read, index, or receive your broader photo library.

The selected image is used only to deliver the Prank feature — that is, to display it on your paired TV. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose. If you do not use the Prank feature, no image is provided to the app.

VVoice Commands & Microphone

OneRemote uses your device's microphone only when you actively start the voice-command feature. The microphone is used solely to capture your spoken command and convert it into text so the app can interpret that text as a command for your TV.

OneRemote does not collect, store, or use your voice recordings for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the voice-command feature. Voice recordings are not retained as part of your OneRemote data.

Only the information necessary to process the voice command is used for the requested feature.

VIThird-Party Services

OneRemote relies on the following third-party providers. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy in addition to ours.

Supabase

Hosts the backend that stores the anonymous installation identifier and related diagnostic records described above, so trial, promotional, and Lifetime entitlements can be managed without user accounts.

Google Play Billing

Processes the Lifetime in-app purchase, purchase restoration, and refunds. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Google AdMob

Serves the optional rewarded advertisements you may choose to watch to unlock temporary access. As part of serving and measuring ads, Google's AdMob SDK may process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and ad interaction events, in accordance with Google's own policies and the consent choices available to you.

VIIData We Do Not Collect

  • We do not ask for or store your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not request precise GPS location. Region information, where used, is derived from your IP address at a coarse level.
  • We do not request continuous access to your photo library — only the single image you explicitly select through the system picker for the Prank feature is received by the app.
  • We do not collect, store, or use your voice recordings for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the voice-command feature. Voice recordings are not retained as part of your OneRemote data.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, or call logs.

VIIIData Security

Data transmitted between the app and our backend is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our backend is restricted, and data stored on our backend is limited to the categories described in this policy. On your device, information such as paired-TV details and settings is stored in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unrooted device.

No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us using the details below.

IXData Retention & Deletion

Information linked to your anonymous installation identifier (trial status, promotional access status, Lifetime entitlement, and related diagnostic records) is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate these features and to prevent abuse of trial and promotional systems.

Voice recordings are not retained as part of your OneRemote data. Voice input exists only long enough to be converted into text for the voice-command feature.

You can request deletion of the data associated with your installation by contacting us at the address in the Contact section. Please include your anonymous device identifier, which is available on the app's Settings screen. After we verify and process your request, we will delete the data associated with that identifier, except where we are required to retain limited records for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or security reasons (for example, records required to substantiate purchase entitlements).

OneRemote does not currently offer traditional user accounts. There is therefore no account-deletion workflow — deletion requests are handled at the installation level as described above.

XChildren's Privacy

OneRemote is a general-audience utility app. It is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law in your region (for example, under 13 in the United States), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

XIChanges to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
